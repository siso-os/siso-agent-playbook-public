Each entry: what broke or what we learned → what changed. The playbook is the residue of these iterations, not a design done in one sitting.

Fresh-agent-per-task becomes law; claude-mini gets fail-closed guards.

Mined 4 corpora (15k+ sessions, 5 harnesses) + the router DB: 66% of all cache-read sat in 128 immortal coordinator sessions; the scarce tier carried 9× the unlimited tier; ~80% idle everywhere; and the fixes already existed, switched off (gateway budgets: 0 rows configured; herdr's zero-token wait primitives: unused). Cross-vendor review (GPT-5.6 Sol) added the deeper root cause: no task-id spine joining routing→execution→artifact→evaluation→budget.
2026-07-10 — the implementation burn. 2026-07-10 — the knowing layer.
